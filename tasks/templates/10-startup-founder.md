# Template 10 — Startup / Founder (`templates/10-startup-founder/`)

## SEO angle
Targets: "resume template for entrepreneurs", "startup resume template html".

## Who it's for
Founders/startup operators who want to lead with impact metrics rather than job titles.

## Layout & sections
Hero with a role/mission statement, a "By the numbers" stat row (e.g. "$2M raised",
"3 exits") placed before Experience.

## Style direction
- Bold accent color; large numeral typography for the stats row.

## Customization points
- Stat row is an easy-to-edit `<ul>` of 3–4 stat/label pairs; can be deleted entirely
  if not applicable to the user.

## Acceptance criteria
- Follows all rules in `../02-design-system.md`.
- Passes `../09-qa-accessibility-checklist.md`.
- Stat row collapses to stacked rows on mobile/print rather than squishing illegibly.
