# Template 12 — Student / Entry-Level (`templates/12-student-entry-level/`)

## SEO angle
Targets: "resume template for students", "entry level resume template html",
"no experience resume template".

## Who it's for
Students/recent grads with little or no work experience.

## Layout & sections
Leads with Education and Projects/Coursework before Experience; "Relevant
Coursework" and "Projects" get equal visual weight to Experience.

## Style direction
- Friendly and approachable — slightly more color than the corporate templates,
  still clean.

## Customization points
- Comments explicitly suggest what to put in a section if it's empty (e.g. "no work
  experience yet? list a class project here instead").

## Acceptance criteria
- Follows all rules in `../02-design-system.md`.
- Passes `../09-qa-accessibility-checklist.md`.
- Designed to comfortably fit one page for this audience's typically shorter content.
