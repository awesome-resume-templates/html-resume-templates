# Template 07 — Timeline (`templates/07-timeline/`)

## SEO angle
Targets: "timeline resume template html".

## Who it's for
People who want to visually emphasize career/education progression over time.

## Layout & sections
Vertical timeline with a connecting line and a dot per entry, used for both
Experience and Education.

## Style direction
- Timeline line/dots rendered in the accent color; dates emphasized on one side of
  each entry.

## Customization points
- Adding an entry = copy one clearly commented timeline-item block.

## Acceptance criteria
- Follows all rules in `../02-design-system.md`.
- Passes `../09-qa-accessibility-checklist.md`.
- In `@media print`, the timeline degrades to a simple stacked list — the connecting
  line is decorative and not required to survive printing.
