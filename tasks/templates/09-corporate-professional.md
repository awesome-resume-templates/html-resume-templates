# Template 09 — Corporate Professional (`templates/09-corporate-professional/`)

## SEO angle
Targets: "professional resume template html", "corporate resume template".

## Who it's for
General corporate/business roles.

## Layout & sections
Two-column grid: main content (Summary, Experience, Education) on the left, a compact
"at a glance" column (Skills, Certifications, Contact) on the right, with a clean
header bar.

## Style direction
- Muted navy/gray palette, structured grid lines, section headers with a subtle
  colored underline.

## Customization points
- Each right-column block is its own `<section>`, so reordering is a simple cut/paste.

## Acceptance criteria
- Follows all rules in `../02-design-system.md`.
- Passes `../09-qa-accessibility-checklist.md`.
