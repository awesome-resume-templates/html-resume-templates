# Template 08 — Elegant Serif (`templates/08-elegant-serif/`)

## SEO angle
Targets: "elegant resume template html", "classic resume template".

## Who it's for
Formal/traditional industries — law, finance, academia-adjacent roles.

## Layout & sections
Centered header, classic single column, thin rule dividers between sections,
generous margins.

## Style direction
- Serif headings, serif or classic sans body text.
- Muted palette (black/cream + one deep accent like burgundy or navy), no rounded
  corners anywhere.

## Customization points
- In-file comments suggest 2–3 free Google Font serif pairings for easy swapping.

## Acceptance criteria
- Follows all rules in `../02-design-system.md`.
- Passes `../09-qa-accessibility-checklist.md`.
