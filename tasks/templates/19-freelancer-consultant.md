# Template 19 — Freelancer / Consultant (`templates/19-freelancer-consultant/`)

## SEO angle
Targets: "freelancer resume template html", "consultant resume template".

## Who it's for
Independent contractors/consultants who need to sell services, not just list a job history.

## Layout & sections
Adds a "Services" section (what they offer) and an optional "Testimonials" block,
alongside the standard Experience section.

## Style direction
- Clean and businesslike, one accent color, quote-style formatting for testimonials.

## Customization points
- Services and Testimonials are both optional/deletable `<section>`s, clearly marked
  as such in comments.

## Acceptance criteria
- Follows all rules in `../02-design-system.md`.
- Passes `../09-qa-accessibility-checklist.md`.
- Testimonials use semantic `<blockquote>`/`<cite>`, not just styled `<div>`s.
